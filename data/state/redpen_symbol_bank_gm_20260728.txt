#0	cable group	# In this specific electrical wiring/control schematic sheet, the symbol represents a multi-conductor composite or multi-pair cable marker with explicit shield/drain wire terminations. Multi-Pair Cable Identification: the rectangular boxes labeled D 6 and 7 combined with the vertical connecting line designate a specific Cable Group or Conductor Bundle. Each horizontal line to the right represents an individual conductor wire within that same physical cable, leading into the controller block (MPCS-S). Twisted Pairs and Wire Naming: G3-22 is the cable label, P stands for Pair and R stands for Return/Reference; the criss-crossed X symbol indicates a Twisted Pair. Cable Shield / Drain Wire Termination: the vertical line with the solid black dots linking the conductors shows a common shielding layer or drain wire tied across these lines; on the far left the heavy vertical bars terminating the R lines indicate Shield Grounding / Chassis Earth Terminations, protecting the low-voltage communication signals going into the LV5 terminals of the controller from electromagnetic noise.
